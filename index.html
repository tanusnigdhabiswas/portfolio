Psychometrics · from scratch

Item Response Theory: Question Calibration & Ability Scoring

  • Built a 2PL IRT model from scratch, calibrating 30 items and scoring 2,500 students jointly from a raw right/wrong response matrix, the statistical engine behind adaptive testing.
  • Used marginal maximum likelihood with EM over 41-point Gauss–Hermite quadrature to integrate out latent ability, avoiding the joint-MLE bias that inflates discrimination estimates.
  • Recovered ground truth at r = 0.98 (difficulty), 0.99 (discrimination) and 0.93 (ability), and caught 3 of 3 planted defective items.

Latent-state modelling

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing: Modelling Skill Mastery

  • Modelled hidden skill mastery across 800 students and 15 practice attempts as a 4-parameter Hidden Markov Model, coding the forward recursion by hand. The same technique drives churn-stage and customer-lifecycle models.
  • Fitted parameters by maximising sequence log-likelihood over 8 bounded restarts with identifiability constraints. All 8 converged to the same optimum, recovering every parameter to within 0.034.
  • Reached 77.2% accuracy and 0.776 AUC predicting the next response for held-out students, with mastery curves estimating attempts needed to clear a 95% mastery threshold.
